# Controlo Approve — Resumo

Resumo is a read/composition surface for understanding the control state of a production.

It is not a persisted parent and has no canonical `resumo_id`.

On the approval side, Resumo may expose the records and states that require review or help the approver understand the production context.

The underlying records retain their own identities and ownership.

Resumo composes them through the real production/control relations; it does not take ownership of them.
